Privacy

AI Job Finder uses your resume and preferences only to search and rank opportunities for you.

No account is required. This app does not ask you to sign in with Google.

The resume, profile, and search results are stored on the computer that runs the API, in a private folder for this browser. Another browser does not receive that folder.

Application links open on the employer’s site. This app does not fill or submit those forms.

You can delete the resume or the saved data from the Profile page. Deleting the saved data removes that browser’s profile, resume, settings, and saved searches from the API host.

Launched from Trident Tech Labs.
