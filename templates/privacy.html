Privacy

AI Job Finder uses your resume and preferences only to search and rank opportunities for you.

Google sign-in uses OpenID. This app asks Google for your name, email, and account id. It does not ask for your Google password, and it does not ask to read your mailbox.

The resume, profile, and search results are stored on the computer that runs this app, in a folder for your account. Another signed-in person does not receive that folder.

Application links open on the employer’s site. This app does not fill or submit those forms.

You can delete the resume or the whole account from the Profile page. Deleting the account removes that account’s profile, resume, settings, and saved searches from this computer.
